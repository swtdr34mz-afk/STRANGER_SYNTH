# STRANGER SYNTH: LINK (SAVE): the USB port plays host to SIX MACHINES; its clock drives the arp, its kick the sidechain
wait 3200
tapkey w0 100
wait 400
tap SAVE
wait 50
expect usbh 1
usbh plug SIX_MACHINES_FM-1
wait 400
expect usbh 2
# the arp on, its notes held; the clock comes from SIX MACHINES
tap ARP
key 4 down 100
wait 50
usbh midi FA 00 00
usbh clock 30
expect arp_ext 1
# the sidechain on its kick
tap ENV
turn K3 60
turn K4 6
expect sc_rate 3
usbh midi 99 24 7F
wait 20
expect sc_duck 1
key 4 up
usbh midi FC 00 00
# LINK off: back to the computer
tap ENV
tap SAVE
wait 50
expect usbh 0
